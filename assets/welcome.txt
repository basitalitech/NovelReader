Welcome to NovelReader

A little reading space on your PS Vita.


Welcome! NovelReader lets you enjoy TXT and EPUB books, discover online novels, and read comfortably on your Vita.

Whether you're starting a new story or continuing an old favorite, your next chapter is just a button away.



Getting Started

To add your own books:

1. Copy your .txt or DRM-free .epub files to your Vita.
2. Open Library → Add Book → Import books.
3. Browse your folders and select your books.
4. Press SQUARE to import your selections.

Your books will appear in Library, ready to read. Original files stay where they are.
You can also explore online novels through Library → Add Book → Online novels.



Your Library

Library keeps all your books in one place, including your favorite online novels.

- CROSS — Open a book.
- SQUARE — Search your library.
- TRIANGLE — Sort or filter books.
- L / R — Switch between main sections.
- CIRCLE — Go back or clear a search.

Select a book to see its details, chapters, and reading progress.
Continue Reading on Home takes you straight back to your latest book.
Your reading progress is saved automatically.



Enjoying Your Books

Once you open a book, you can read using buttons or the touchscreen.

- LEFT / RIGHT — Turn pages.
- UP / DOWN — Scroll through text.
- CROSS — Open the chapter list.
- SQUARE — Add or remove a bookmark.
- SELECT — Jump to your latest bookmark.
- START — Open reading controls.
- CIRCLE — Leave the reader.

For EPUB books, L / R switches chapters.
You can also swipe left or right to turn pages, scroll by dragging, or tap the center to hide or show the reading bars.



Make Reading Your Own

Open START → Reader Settings to customize your reading experience.
Choose your favorite font, adjust text size and spacing, change themes, or try different page-turn effects.
You can preview your changes before applying them.
NovelReader remembers your preferences, so every book feels just right.



Bookmarks & Chapters

Found a passage you love? Press SQUARE while reading to bookmark it.
Visit Bookmarks from the sidebar to see your saved places, or press SELECT while reading to return to your latest bookmark.
To jump between chapters, press CROSS while reading and choose where you'd like to go.
You can also use START → Find in book to search for a word or passage.



Pictures & Read Aloud

Books with illustrations let you view pictures up close.
Tap an illustration to open it. Pinch to zoom, drag to move around, or use the controller buttons.
Want to listen instead? Open START → Read aloud (TTS) → Start here to have your book read aloud using the built-in English voice, even offline.



Discover Online Novels

Connect your Vita to Wi-Fi and open Library → Add Book → Online novels.
Browse stories from supported sources, search for your favorites, and start reading.
You can add novels to your Library and download available chapters for offline reading.
Downloaded chapters remain available without Wi-Fi. Some online sources may occasionally be unavailable.



A Few Helpful Tips

- Your reading position saves automatically.
- Moving or renaming a book? Use Locate file in its details to reconnect it.
- Removing a book from Library clears its saved progress and bookmarks, but keeps your original TXT or EPUB file.
- Most menus can be controlled using either buttons or touch.
- Press CIRCLE whenever you want to go back.
- Open START for additional options.



Happy Reading!

Welcome to Novel Reader

A little library for your Vita

Copy UTF-8 .txt novels or DRM-free .epub books into ux0:data/NovelReader/books/ using VitaShell, then press TRIANGLE in the library to refresh.

Press CROSS to open a book. Your last reading position is restored automatically. Books stay on your memory card and work entirely offline.

In the library, SQUARE cycles Title, Recent, and Progress sorting. L and R switch between All books, Reading, and Finished. Your sort and filter are saved. EPUB titles and authors are read during scanning; the selected book's cover appears beside the list.

You can organize multiple books into folders inside ux0:data/NovelReader/books/. Folders appear before books. Press CROSS or tap a folder to open it, then choose any book inside. Nested folders work too. CIRCLE or the touch Up button returns to the parent folder. Returning from a book keeps you in its folder.

Reading and Finished filters apply to books in the current folder. Folders stay visible under every filter so you can always browse deeper. Each book's save uses its relative path, so Series A/Volume 1.epub and Series B/Volume 1.epub have separate progress and bookmarks. Moving a book or renaming its folder starts a new save identity. Existing root-level saves still work.

Library progress bars show saved reading progress. EPUB percentages estimate progress by weighting chapters equally. Finished means the final page was displayed. Moving back to an earlier page updates that status.

Getting comfortable

Either analog stick can navigate lists and the reader. Move vertically to select items or move through text; move horizontally to browse lists or turn pages. Return the stick to center after changing screens. In the image viewer, either stick pans smoothly.

Use LEFT and RIGHT to turn pages. UP and DOWN move one text line or illustration at a time. Hold a direction to keep moving. In TXT books, the shoulder buttons also turn pages.

In EPUB books, the shoulder buttons open the previous or next chapter. CROSS opens the contents menu. Choose a chapter with UP and DOWN, browse with LEFT and RIGHT, then press CROSS to open it. CIRCLE returns to reading. Page turning also crosses chapter boundaries automatically.

Press TRIANGLE while reading to change the font size, line spacing, or theme. Choose Night, Paper, or Sepia. Your position is preserved when the text reflows.

Press SQUARE to set a bookmark. Press SELECT to jump back to it, including across EPUB chapters. Each book has one bookmark in addition to its automatically saved reading position.

Press CIRCLE to return to your library. Press START to save and exit. Reading positions are saved as you move, so there is no separate save menu.

Using the touchscreen

Tap a book in the library to open it. Swipe lists to browse six entries at a time. The buttons along the bottom of every screen work by touch, including sorting, filtering, bookmarks, settings, and exit.

While reading, swipe left for the next page and right for the previous page. Swipe vertically to move through the text. Tap an inline illustration to open its zoom viewer. Other taps on the left and right sides turn pages; a center tap opens contents. Hold and release on the reading area to save a bookmark.

In settings, tap a row to select it, then use the Decrease, Increase, and Apply buttons. Rear touch is not used.

Preparing your books

This version reads plain UTF-8 text, with or without a UTF-8 byte order mark. Both Windows and Unix line endings are supported. TXT books may be up to 8 MiB each. Refresh rescans the current folder after you copy or rename files. The reader starts at the library root when launched.

EPUB chapters load one at a time in the book's reading order. The reader preserves paragraphs, headings, bold text, and underlined emphasis. Chapter documents must be well-formed UTF-8 XHTML and at most 2 MiB each.

Viewing illustrations

Illustrations now appear between the surrounding paragraphs as you read. Small images share the page with text, and large illustrations get a page of their own. Images fit the reading area without cropping or stretching.

While reading an EPUB, press CROSS for contents, then SQUARE for illustrations in the current chapter. Choose an image and press CROSS to view it. Your reading position and bookmark stay unchanged.

In the viewer, CROSS zooms in, SQUARE zooms out, and the D-pad pans. TRIANGLE fits the image to the screen. L and R show the previous and next image in the chapter. CIRCLE returns to the screen that opened the viewer.

You can also drag an image to pan and pinch to zoom. Tap the image to toggle between fit and 2x zoom, or use the touch toolbar. Back returns to reading if you opened the image by tapping it inline, or to the image list if you opened it there.

PNG and JPEG are supported, up to 8 MiB encoded, 4096 pixels per side, and 4 megapixels total. Missing, damaged, or unsupported images show a labeled placeholder. Existing reading positions and bookmarks still work, including positions on illustrations.

Vertical writing, CSS layouts, and ruby annotations are not implemented yet. Font coverage depends on the Vita system font. Convert other formats to UTF-8 TXT before copying them here.

The next chapter

The reader core is separate from the Vita interface. Library caching, more image formats, and search are future local improvements. Online catalogues and Lua source parsers are planned for future work.

Until then, find a comfortable spot and enjoy your book.

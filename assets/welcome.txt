Welcome to Novel Reader

A little library for your Vita

Copy UTF-8 .txt novels or DRM-free .epub books into ux0:data/NovelReader/books/ using VitaShell, then tap Import in Library or choose Import books in TRIANGLE's options panel. Browse to a book and press CROSS to add it to your collection.

Press CROSS to open a book. Your last reading position is restored automatically. Books stay on your memory card and work entirely offline.

In the library, SQUARE opens a chooser for Title, Recent, and Progress sorting. L and R switch between the visible All, Reading, and Finished tabs; you can also tap them. Your sort and filter are saved. Four large book rows appear per page, with the selected book's cover thumbnail. Info shows the full title, cover, author, and chapter count. Tap Continue reading, or choose it in Options, to resume your most recently read book.

Library shows your imported books and favorite online novels together, without folders. The Import browser lets you browse folders inside ux0:data/NovelReader/books/. CROSS opens a folder or imports a book. CIRCLE goes up a folder, or returns to Library at the import root. Tap Refresh after copying files. Imports keep files in place and repeated imports do not add duplicates.

Each book keeps its relative path for progress and bookmarks. Series A/Volume 1.epub and Series B/Volume 1.epub have independent saves. Existing saves still work after import. Moving or renaming a book changes its save identity; restore missing files to their imported locations. The collection is saved in ux0:data/NovelReader/library.txt.

Library progress bars show saved reading progress. EPUB percentages estimate progress by weighting chapters equally. Finished means the final page was displayed. Moving back to an earlier page updates that status.

Getting comfortable

Reading starts in immersive mode, with more room for text and illustrations. Tap the center of the text or press TRIANGLE for a large reading control panel. Opening and closing it overlays the same page without reflowing. Appearance lets you choose a page-only view or a persistent reading bar; this choice is saved across restarts. Changing the view preserves the current text/image anchor and bookmark.

Press TRIANGLE or tap Options for a control panel. L and R together are an optional shortcut. Use the D-pad or either stick to choose a tile, CROSS to confirm, and CIRCLE to close. Common features also have direct, large touch buttons. Disabled actions are dimmed. Brief confirmations disappear automatically; errors stay until navigation or another action replaces them. Menus keep their neutral charcoal colors independently of your reading theme.

Either analog stick can navigate lists and the reader. Move vertically to select items or move through text; move horizontally to browse lists or turn pages. Return the stick to center after changing screens. In the image viewer, either stick pans smoothly.

Use LEFT and RIGHT to turn pages. UP and DOWN move one text line or illustration at a time. Hold a direction to keep moving. In TXT books, the shoulder buttons also turn pages.

In EPUB books, the shoulder buttons open the previous or next chapter. CROSS opens the contents menu. Choose a chapter with UP and DOWN, browse with LEFT and RIGHT, then press CROSS to open it. The reading chapter has its own label, separate from the focused row. Tap Jump, or choose Jump to chapter in Options, to enter a chapter number. CIRCLE returns to reading. Page turning also crosses chapter boundaries automatically.

Press TRIANGLE while reading, then choose Appearance. Text has font size, reading font, and line spacing; Display has the reading theme and reading view. L and R switch tabs. Choose Literata for serif reading, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for sans-serif reading, or Vita system. Menus use Source Sans 3. Preferences are saved across restarts. Night, Paper, and Sepia apply to the reading page and preview. Your position is preserved when the text reflows.

Press SQUARE to set a bookmark. Press SELECT to jump back to it, including across EPUB chapters. Each book has one bookmark in addition to its automatically saved reading position.

Press CIRCLE to return to your library. Press START to save and exit. Reading positions are saved as you move, so there is no separate save menu.

Using the touchscreen

Tap a book in the library to open it. Swipe lists to browse four entries at a time, or use the large paging arrows. Large buttons along the bottom work by touch. Options opens a control panel. A center tap while reading opens the reading controls.

While reading, swipe left for the next page and right for the previous page. Swipe vertically to move through the text. Tap an inline illustration to open its zoom viewer. Other taps on the left and right sides turn pages; a center tap opens the reading control panel. Use CROSS or the Contents action for chapters. Hold and release on the reading area to save a bookmark.

In Appearance, tap a row to select it, then its large minus/plus buttons to adjust. Tap a value to cycle it. In Display, tap Night, Paper, or Sepia swatches to change theme. The live preview shows your chosen reading font and spacing. Apply returns to your reading position. Rear touch is not used.

Preparing your books

This version reads plain UTF-8 text, with or without a UTF-8 byte order mark. Both Windows and Unix line endings are supported. TXT books may be up to 8 MiB each. Use Import to add files after copying them. The reader starts in your saved Library when launched.

EPUB chapters load one at a time in the book's reading order. The reader preserves paragraphs, headings, bold text, and underlined emphasis. Chapter documents must be well-formed UTF-8 XHTML and at most 2 MiB each.

Viewing illustrations

Illustrations now appear between the surrounding paragraphs as you read. Small images share the page with text, and large illustrations get a page of their own. Images fit the reading area without cropping or stretching.

While reading an EPUB, press CROSS for contents, then SQUARE for illustrations in the current chapter. Choose an image and press CROSS to view it. Your reading position and bookmark stay unchanged.

In the viewer, CROSS zooms in, SQUARE zooms out, and the D-pad pans. Fit has a direct touch button and is also available in Options. L and R show the previous and next image in the chapter. CIRCLE returns to the screen that opened the viewer. All six actions are on the touch bar.

You can also drag an image to pan and pinch to zoom. Tap the image to toggle between fit and 2x zoom, or use the touch toolbar. Back returns to reading if you opened the image by tapping it inline, or to the image list if you opened it there.

PNG and JPEG are supported, up to 8 MiB encoded, 4096 pixels per side, and 4 megapixels total. Missing, damaged, or unsupported images show a labeled placeholder. Existing reading positions and bookmarks still work, including positions on illustrations.

Vertical writing, CSS layouts, and ruby annotations are not implemented yet. Missing characters in the selected font fall back to the Vita system font. Complex-script shaping is not supported. Convert other formats to UTF-8 TXT before copying them here.

The next chapter

Press SELECT in the library, or tap Online, to browse public novels from Novgo, Asura Novels, and Beast Novels. Connect Wi-Fi in Vita Settings first. Asura uses novels, not comics.

Choose a source, then use SQUARE to search with the system keyboard. Open a novel with CROSS for details, and press CROSS again for its chapters. CROSS reads and saves a chapter. SQUARE downloads the selected chapter, and SELECT downloads all public chapters. Locked chapters require login or purchase and are not downloaded in this version.

While a request runs, CIRCLE cancels it. The progress bar shows completed downloads; completed chapters are kept. After a failure, tap Retry, choose Retry request in Options, or repeat a download action. Completed chapters are skipped. START cancels, saves, and exits. Online chapter lists expose Read, Download, All, and Jump directly.

On a novel's details screen, SQUARE or the Save button saves it as a favorite alongside your local books. Browsing and downloading do not automatically add a favorite. Open a saved novel from Library for its details, offline count, and Read or Resume action. SELECT starts or resumes reading; CROSS opens chapters with Offline and Locked labels. Download all and Refresh chapters have direct buttons. Use UP and DOWN to read longer descriptions. The usual reader controls, settings, bookmarks, and chapter navigation work. Missing public chapters download on demand when Wi-Fi is connected.

Online chapters are plain text with paragraph breaks. Website layout changes or browser verification can temporarily stop a source; the app shows an error and keeps downloaded chapters. Library caching, more image formats, and local text search remain future improvements.

Until then, find a comfortable spot and enjoy your book.
